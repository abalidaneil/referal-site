
Onboarding & First Steps
First Recruit – Made your first referral

Welcome Wagon – Referred someone who completed a signup

Link Dropper – Shared your referral link for the first time

Foot in the Door – Got a click on your referral link

Quantity-Based Streaks
Social Butterfly – 5 referrals

Connector – 10 referrals

Magnet – 25 referrals

Super Connector – 50 referrals

Legend – 100 referrals

Walking Billboard – 250+ referrals

Quality-Based Tiers
Quality Seeker – 3 referrals who made a purchase

The Influencer – 5 referrals who each bring 2 more people

High Roller – Highest average order value from referrals

Team Player – Referred someone who became a top referrer

Speed & Streaks
Fast Starter – First referral within 24 hours

Hot Streak – Referred someone 3 days in a row

Unstoppable – Referred someone 7 days in a row

Weekly Warrior – 5 referrals in one week

Daily Driver – At least 1 referral every day for a week

Social Sharing Actions
Share & Shout – Shared link on 3 different platforms

Hashtag Hero – Used your referral code in a social post

Click Captain – 100 clicks on your shared link

QR Ranger – Someone scanned your QR code to join

Milestone Celebrations
Bronze Booster – 5 total referred friends

Silver Supporter – 25 total referred friends

Gold Greeter – 50 total referred friends

Platinum Partner – 100 total referred friends

Diamond Diplomat – 250 total referred friends

Faction / Leaderboard (Competitive)
Top Recruit (Weekly) – Most referral signups this week

Conversion King – Highest referral conversion rate

Repeat Offender – Someone you referred also made a referral

The Hive Mind – Your referrals have referred others (2nd-degree network)

Fun / Themed Badges
Eagle Eye – Caught a bug or gave useful feedback

Night Owl – Made a referral between 12 AM – 5 AM

Early Bird – First referral of the day

Completionist – Earned all badges in one category

OG (Original Gangster) – Among first 100 users to join
